We only process personal data that is relevant for providing and improving the app, administering your subscription, offering support and complying with applicable law. Which data this is depends on the features you use.

4 — Purposes and legal bases

Creating and running your account To create and administer your user profile and give access to the app's features. GDPR art. 6(1)(b) — performance of a contract.

Subscription To give access to subscription features, keep track of subscription status and free trial, and handle renewal and expiry. GDPR art. 6(1)(b). Bookkeeping data is also processed under art. 6(1)(c).

Personalised content in the app To show relevant content about pregnancy, baby sleep, development and developmental leaps based on what you enter, and to calculate the sleep log and milestones. GDPR art. 6(1)(b). Where the data constitutes health data or other special categories, it is only processed with explicit consent under GDPR art. 9(2)(a).

A light in the dark To show how many other users are awake and roughly where, so that you can see you are not alone. GDPR art. 6(1)(a) — consent. The feature is off until you switch it on yourself, and can be switched off again at any time.

Messages and community To make it possible to write to each other, to block and report, and to moderate misuse. GDPR art. 6(1)(b) and (f) — contract, and our legitimate interest in a safe community.

Expert bookings To carry out the booking, share the necessary information with the chosen expert and provide follow-up. GDPR art. 6(1)(b). Sensitive data is only processed if you choose to share it yourself and the relevant consent is in place.

Support To answer questions, resolve technical problems and document our dialogue with you. GDPR art. 6(1)(b) and (f).

Operation, security and troubleshooting To protect the app against misuse, analyse faults, ensure stable operation and prevent fraud. GDPR art. 6(1)(f) — legitimate interest in secure and stable operation.

Marketing To send newsletters, offers and inspiration if you have given consent. GDPR art. 6(1)(a) — consent, which you can always withdraw.

Compliance with the law To meet bookkeeping requirements and handle objections, claims or enquiries from public authorities. GDPR art. 6(1)(c) and (f).

5 — Data about pregnancy, children and health

Club No Sleep is aimed at pregnant women and parents. Some of the information you choose to enter may therefore concern pregnancy, your child's age, sleep, wellbeing or development.

Such information may in some cases be sensitive personal data under the GDPR. We only process it where it is necessary for the feature you are using and where there is a valid legal basis — typically your explicit consent.

  • You decide yourself what information you enter in the app.
  • Do not share sensitive information in the community features if you do not want other users to see it.
  • If you enter information about a child, you confirm that you are the parent or guardian, or otherwise entitled to provide that information.
  • The app is not intended for children to create accounts or use the service themselves without an adult.

The app's AI-based sleep guidance gives general answers based on the information you have entered yourself. The answers are not healthcare advice and do not replace a doctor, midwife or health visitor.

6 — «A light in the dark» and location

«A light in the dark» shows where other parents in your country are awake. This is how it works:

  • The feature is off until you switch it on yourself, and you are asked before the phone requests location permission.
  • Your location is rounded to roughly one kilometre before it is stored. We never store your exact position.
  • Other users only see a light on the map — not your address, your name or your precise location, unless you choose to give it in a message.
  • Your light is only on while you have a sleep log running, and switches off automatically no later than two hours after your last activity.
  • You can switch the feature off at any time under Profile, and your location is removed from the map immediately.

10 — Storage and deletion

We keep personal data for as long as it is necessary for the purposes it was collected for, or for as long as we are required to by law. When data is no longer necessary, it is deleted or anonymised.

Account and profile data Kept for as long as your account is active. When an account is deleted, the data is deleted or anonymised within a reasonable time, unless there is a lawful basis for keeping it longer.

Subscription and bookkeeping Data that constitutes bookkeeping material is kept for 5 years from the end of the financial year it relates to.

Sleep log, milestones and photos Kept for as long as your account is active, and deleted when the account is deleted. You can delete individual entries yourself in the app.

Location Stored only while the feature is switched on, and overwritten each time a new reading is recorded. If you switch the feature off, the location is removed.

Messages and community content Messages are kept for as long as both parties' accounts are active. When an account is deleted, your content is deleted or anonymised. In some cases content may still appear in anonymised form where it forms part of a conversation with another user.

Support enquiries Kept for as long as necessary to handle the enquiry and document the course of events.

Marketing consent Kept until you withdraw your consent. Documentation of consent may be kept for a period in order to be able to document lawful marketing.

Technical logs and security data Kept only for as long as necessary for operation, troubleshooting, security and the prevention of misuse. Longer retention may occur in the case of a specific security incident or dispute.

Deletion from backups takes place in accordance with our ordinary backup and security procedures.

12 — Cookies, analytics and similar technologies

On clubnosleep.com, cookies are used to make the site work and — if you give consent — for statistics and marketing. You can change your choice at any time in the cookie settings at the bottom of the page.

In the app itself, local storage is used to keep you logged in and remember your settings. No marketing tracking is used in the app without your consent.

We measure how our advertising performs using Meta. This only happens if you have said yes. On iPhone and iPad we ask first with our own message and then with Apple's tracking dialog. If you say no in either place, we send no advertising identifier to Meta, and the app works exactly as before.

If you say yes, we tell Meta that the app has been installed, that a profile has been created, and that a subscription has started or renewed, along with the amount. We never send health data, information about your child, sleep logs, messages or photos to Meta.

This processing rests on your consent, GDPR art. 6(1)(a). You can withdraw your consent in your phone's settings under Privacy & Security → Tracking, and on Android under the Google settings for ads.

Meta is an independent data controller for the data it receives. Their own privacy policy is at facebook.com/privacy/policy.
